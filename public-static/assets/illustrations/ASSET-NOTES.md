# UI illustrations

- `isometric-pixel-city.png` is a newly generated original pixel-art illustration for the shared page headers, dashboard, and sidebar. It takes general industrial skyline and steel-frame themes from the user-provided references; the Dreamstime watermarked image itself is not included in the app.
- User-provided icon packs include industrial construction and stage-equipment illustrations. The generated city incorporates small construction-site and scaffolding motifs from those references.

- `management-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration for the dashboard management hub. It combines the user's scaffolded-building, city/drawing, and worksite-management references into one wide scene: a fenced scaffold-rental warehouse with stock racks, forklift, trucks, HIAB, lifts, a road to an active scaffolded city worksite, residential buildings between work zones, a glass-fronted CRM office beside a market-research office, training and legal buildings, an outer-edge factory, a large blueprint surface, and visible sun, clouds, and rain. Clickable overlays open WMS, RENT, LMS, training/certification, HRMS, legal/audit/compliance, CRMS, Markkina- ja hankintavahti, MMS, design/CAD, scaffold management, GeoBIM, and live weather/time.
- `management-isometric-weatherless.png` is a derived version of the management hub illustration with the fixed sun, cloud, and rain symbols removed. The dashboard weather widget supplies the single current weather icon instead.
- `management-isometric-logistics-weatherless.png` is the updated dashboard and Dev UI map. It separates the WMS stock yard, RENT equipment yard, dedicated slate-blue LMS dispatch apron with a box truck, scaffold flatbed, HIAB truck, and additional delivery vehicles, and the cream drawing pad. It has no fixed weather symbols because the widget shows one live condition icon.
- `crm-glass-office-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration for the CRMS page, showing a glass-fronted customer meeting office in the same isometric style.
- `hrms-people-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration for the HRMS page, showing a people-operations and training pavilion, PPE, and a crew safety briefing.
- `rental-yard-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration for the construction-equipment rental page, with a fenced plant yard, boom and scissor lifts, scaffold gear, and a prominent RENT sign.
- `market-intelligence-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration for Markkina- ja hankintavahti, with a public-source monitoring office next to the CRM customer office.
- `manufacturing-factory-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration for MMS, with an industrial production hall, materials, quality checks, loading, and supplier offices.
- `legal-compliance-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration for the legal, audit, compliance, and certification area, with a civic courthouse-style office and records team.
- `design-planning-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration used on the GeoBIM/CAD planning page. Its prompt paired a facade and scaffold with a blueprint sheet and drafting tools.
- `maps-drawings-isometric.png` is an original generated transparent illustration used on the GeoBIM and CAD/blueprint archive pages. Its prompt paired a street map, selected site, building footprint, blueprint, scale, and pencil.
- `logistics-warehouse-isometric.png` is the workspace copy of the logistics/warehouse illustration selected from the user's supplied visual reference. It is shared by WMS and LMS logistics.
- `../icons/weather/*.svg` contains the compact original sun, moon, cloud, fog, rain, snow, storm, and unknown-state icons used by the single live-weather widget.

The generated illustrations do not contain third-party text, logos, or watermarks. The selected logistics image is a workspace-supplied illustration.
