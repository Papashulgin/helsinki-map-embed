Source index: https://telinekataja.fi/rakennustelineet/esitteet-ja-ohjeet/

The 2018 manual is an assembly/use guide, not a design approval for a specific scaffold. The catalogue's dimensions identify components and nominal system dimensions; they do not provide a project-specific structural design. Follow the manual's safety requirements and have the scaffold-specific stability, loads, ties, base support and bracing checked by a qualified designer.
